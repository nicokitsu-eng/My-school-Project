Wildlife of the Bicol Region

Bicol is home to forests, volcanic mountains, rivers, grasslands, mangroves, and productive coastal waters. These habitats support remarkable Philippine wildlife, including species with extremely restricted ranges as well as animals that have become major attractions for ecotourism.

Native Mammal

Philippine Brown Deer

Rusa marianna

A slender Philippine deer with a dark brown coat and short antlers. Deer occur in suitable forest and grassland habitats in the region, although hunting and habitat loss have reduced their populations.

Popular Wildlife Tourist Attractions

Some of Bicol's most famous wildlife experiences are marine encounters, birdwatching destinations, and cultural attractions.

Bicol's Famous Marine Attraction

Whale Shark

Rhincodon typus • "Butanding"

Donsol in Sorsogon is internationally known for whale shark encounters. These enormous filter-feeding sharks can reach extraordinary sizes and seasonally occur in waters around the region.

Marine Attraction

Giant Manta Ray

Mobula birostris

Giant manta rays are spectacular pelagic rays associated with productive waters and cleaning stations. The Manta Bowl area is famous among divers for manta encounters.

Protecting Bicol's Wildlife

Bicol's biodiversity depends on the protection of forests, watersheds, rivers, coastal habitats, mangroves, and marine ecosystems. Responsible tourism should minimize disturbance to wildlife and support local conservation programs and communities.
